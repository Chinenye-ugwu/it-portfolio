# Week 7 – What’s Next?

## Introduction

Week 7 concludes my IT497 Capstone journey by giving me an opportunity to look back at the goals I established at the beginning of the course and consider how those goals have developed.

Throughout the course, I learned that professional growth is not limited to technical knowledge. My development has involved technical skills, spiritual growth, ethical decision-making, service, self-awareness, and preparation for my future career.

My goal remains to continue developing toward a career in System Administration or Cloud Support while becoming an IT professional who can be trusted with technology, information, and responsibility.

## My Original Goals

At the beginning of my IT497 journey, I identified several important goals:

- Develop practical Information Technology skills.
- Prepare for a career in System Administration or Cloud Support.
- Strengthen my understanding of cloud computing, networking, cybersecurity, Linux, and system administration.
- Develop a professional identity based on integrity and responsibility.
- Continue growing spiritually while developing professionally.

These goals provided direction for my academic and professional development throughout the course.

## Progress Toward My Goals

During the course, I made progress in both technical and personal areas.

My technical development included experiences with:

- Cloud computing
- System administration
- Linux
- Networking
- Cybersecurity
- Troubleshooting
- Security monitoring
- Technical documentation

My portfolio also became a place where I could organize evidence of my academic and technical development.

More importantly, I learned that technical ability needs to be supported by strong character. My reflections helped me recognize the importance of patience, humility, integrity, service, responsibility, and continuous learning.

## How My Goals Evolved

My original career direction has remained consistent. I continue to see System Administration and Cloud Support as important career goals.

However, my definition of professional success has changed.

I now understand that becoming an IT professional is not only about gaining technical skills or obtaining employment. It also involves being trustworthy, ethical, responsible, adaptable, and willing to serve others.

My goal has therefore developed from simply becoming an IT professional to becoming a trustworthy IT professional whose technical abilities are guided by strong character.

## Lessons I Am Taking Forward

One of the most important lessons from this course is that challenges can become opportunities for growth.

Technical problems taught me patience and perseverance. Spiritual reflection helped me recognize how I respond when I feel frustrated or overwhelmed. Peer feedback taught me the importance of listening and being willing to improve.

I also learned that continuous learning is essential in Information Technology because technology, security practices, and professional expectations continue to change.

## My Future Direction

My future development will continue to be guided by three principles:

### Integrity

I will continue developing the ability to handle systems, information, and administrative responsibilities ethically and responsibly.

### Service

I will use my technical knowledge to support users, organizations, coworkers, and others who depend on technology.

### Continuous Learning

I will continue developing my knowledge through education, hands-on projects, professional training, and practical experience.

## Continuing My Professional Growth

My portfolio will continue to develop after IT497.

As I progress through my Information Technology education and career preparation, I can add:

- New technical projects
- Certifications
- Professional experiences
- New technical skills
- Additional cloud and system administration experience
- Future reflections and accomplishments

The portfolio will remain a record of both what I have learned and how I continue to grow.

## Final Reflection

IT497 has helped me understand that spiritual growth and professional growth can strengthen one another.

I want to continue growing as a person, a disciple of Jesus Christ, and an IT professional. My faith provides principles that can guide my decisions, while my education and professional experiences provide opportunities to apply those principles.

As I move forward, I will continue building my technical abilities while remaining committed to integrity, service, and continuous learning.

My journey does not end with IT497. This is the beginning of the next stage of my professional and personal development.

## Roundtable Discussion

I participated in the Week 7 roundtable discussion with my peers and shared my Spiritual Progression and Professional Growth Portfolio.

During the discussion, I reflected on how IT497 helped me connect my faith, previous professional experience, and developing IT career. I shared how my ten years of experience in insurance helped me understand the importance of confidentiality, client trust, documentation, and accountability, and how these principles continue to influence my development as an IT professional.

I also discussed how projects such as my Nmap network reconnaissance and Heritage Vault security architecture helped me develop patience, careful documentation, systematic problem-solving, and greater awareness of the ethical responsibilities associated with technology.

### Feedback I Received

During the roundtable, I received feedback on my portfolio from a peer who reviewed my website.

The feedback identified several strengths. My portfolio was described as clear and professional, with well-organized sections including About, Résumé, Skills, Projects, Certifications, Career Goals, and Contact. The reviewer also recognized my career transition from insurance to Information Technology as a compelling part of my professional story and noted the relevance of my technical content in areas such as Linux, Azure, AWS, networking, and cybersecurity.

The reviewer also recognized that my updated reflections were authentic and specific, particularly my connections between projects such as Nmap and Heritage Vault and values such as integrity and service. The reviewer noted that my honest acknowledgment of areas where I am still developing strengthened the credibility of my portfolio.

The main suggestions for improvement were to strengthen the visual design, add more concrete project evidence such as screenshots, diagrams, and short demonstrations, and continue developing the certifications section.

This feedback reinforced the importance of continuing to improve both the technical evidence and visual presentation of my portfolio.

### Feedback I Provided to Other Students

I also provided constructive feedback on several peers' portfolios.

For **Esther**, I recognized the strength of her written reflection, her connection between guiding principles and specific IT applications, her usability testing, and her ethical dilemma analysis. I suggested that she share the actual live Google Sites portfolio rather than only the folder containing the source documents. I also recommended creating a single accessible landing page so viewers would not have to open multiple separate files to understand her work.

For **Alimamy Sankoh**, I recognized the strength of his extensive telecommunications background, his ethical leadership case study based on a real workplace incident, and the substantial professional content in his portfolio. I recommended publishing the portfolio, adding project evidence to the empty gallery, and breaking up dense paragraphs with headings or other formatting to make the portfolio easier to navigate.

For **Mbah**, I recognized the strong connection between the CIA Triad and stewardship principles, the clear before-and-after reflection structure, the action plan, and the use of supporting sources. I suggested making the portfolio easier to view than a downloadable PowerPoint and adding named technical projects with specific tools and outcomes to demonstrate hands-on experience.

Providing this feedback helped me practice evaluating technical and professional work from another person's perspective. I tried to identify strengths first and then provide specific recommendations that could realistically improve each portfolio.

### Key Insights From the Discussion

The roundtable reinforced for me that professional growth is not only about completing assignments. It also involves being willing to receive feedback, evaluate my own work honestly, and learn from the experiences of others.

One important insight I gained was that **evidence matters**. A portfolio can describe skills and experiences, but screenshots, project demonstrations, technical documentation, and concrete outcomes make those experiences more credible and easier for others to understand.

I also recognized that presentation and usability are important parts of professional communication. Technical knowledge needs to be communicated in a way that employers, users, instructors, and other professionals can understand.

The discussion also reinforced the connection between spiritual values and professional responsibilities. Across the portfolios we discussed, themes such as integrity, service, responsible stewardship, ethical decision-making, and continuous learning appeared repeatedly. This reminded me that these principles can guide how I use technology and interact with others throughout my career.

### Reflection on My Presentation

The feedback I received about my portfolio presentation helped me recognize that my work has a strong foundation but can continue to improve. In particular, the recommendation to include stronger visual evidence and demonstrations gives me a clear direction for future updates.

I will continue improving my portfolio by adding technical project evidence, strengthening its visual presentation, and documenting future certifications and practical experiences.

# Business Intelligence & SQL Analytics

Coursework from IT 340 (Business Intelligence Systems) at Ensign College. I used SQL to answer business questions on three datasets, then presented the results to non-technical audiences using Tableau and Power BI dashboards and written executive summaries.

## Datasets and Questions

- **JFK Airport:** Which plane model is used most? Which carriers have the highest share of windy departures? How does rain relate to departure delays?
- **MLB (Lahman Baseball Database):** Who are the best pitchers and batters? Which teams had the best records recently? Which schools produce the best players?
- **Chinook Music Store:** Who manages each employee? Which artists have the most songs? Which artists show the strongest revenue growth? How should tracks be renumbered on a new multidisc album?

## What's in This Project

- [dashboards](dashboards): Tableau and Power BI dashboards for all three datasets
- [executive-summaries](executive-summaries): nine written summaries with the SQL, metric rationale, limitations, and extra data needed

## Tools and Skills

- SQL (joins, CTEs, window functions), DataGrip, PostgreSQL
- Tableau and Power BI dashboards
- Executive summary writing for non-technical audiences

## Selected Findings

- Babe Ruth ranks first in career OPS (1.164) among batters with at least 3,000 at-bats.
- Endeavor Air has the highest share of windy departures at JFK (8.31%).
- Rainy departures at JFK averaged 113.1 minutes of delay versus 12.6 in clear weather, but only 18 flights occurred in rain, so the result should be read with caution.
- Iron Maiden has the most songs in the Chinook catalog (213 tracks).

# Week 5 – Integration, Reflection, and Design Enhancement

This week focused on integrating spiritual growth with professional development, improving the usability of my Professional Growth Portfolio, reflecting on ethical decision-making, and connecting my faith with my future professional practice.

## Week 5 Portfolio Work

### Task 1 – Spiritual Portfolio Enhancement

This reflection examines the integration of Christlike attributes into my personal and professional life. It also identifies personal warning signs and strategies I can use to realign myself when I become discouraged, impatient, overwhelmed, or disconnected from my spiritual goals.

[View Task 1 – Spiritual Portfolio Enhancement](week5-task1.md)

### Task 2A – Usability Testing and Design Enhancements

This report documents the usability testing I conducted on my Professional Growth Portfolio using both my mobile phone and laptop. The testing included reviewing navigation, checking that sections displayed their appropriate content, and identifying and correcting an issue on the Contact page where LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio URLs appeared as plain text rather than clickable links.

[View Task 2A – Usability Testing and Design Enhancements](week5-task2a.md)

This reflection examines the ethical dilemma from Week 2 and explains my personal view of the situation, how I would respond to similar situations in my future IT career, and how the experience strengthened my understanding of privacy, consent, integrity, responsibility, and ethical decision-making.

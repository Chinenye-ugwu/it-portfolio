POLICY FAQs (Sample / Dummy Data for Learning Purposes)

Q: What happens if a client misses a premium payment?
A: There is a 30-day grace period from the due date. If payment is not
received within that window, the policy lapses and must be reinstated,
which may require a new health declaration for life policies.

Q: Can a client change their payment frequency (monthly to annual)?
A: Yes, payment frequency can be changed at the next renewal date by
submitting a written request at least 14 days before renewal.

Q: What documents are required to file a claim?
A: A completed claim form, valid ID, the original policy document, and
for motor claims, a police report if the incident involved a third party.

Q: How long does claim processing typically take?
A: Standard claims are processed within 10 working days of receiving
complete documentation. Complex claims may take longer pending
investigation.
